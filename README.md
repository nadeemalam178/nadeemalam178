### 🏪 Flagship Project: [Shalimar Store (v3.2.4)](https://github.com/nadeemalam178/shalimar-store)

> **Since 1988 — Trust & Quality General Store Mobile App**  
> *Alamganj, Ashok Rajpath, Patna - 800007, Bihar*

**[Shalimar Store](https://github.com/nadeemalam178/shalimar-store)** is a production-grade, dual-mode general store & grocery mobile application built with Flutter & Dart. It brings traditional neighborhood shopping into the digital era with **zero gateway fees**, **compulsory customer registration & active Google sign-in**, **direct WhatsApp ordering**, **zero-fee UPI payments**, **Google Firebase real-time cloud catalog sync**, **in-app direct OTA updates**, and an **ID & Password protected owner management panel**.

- ⚡ **Dual-Mode Architecture:** Customer catalog browsing + Owner/Admin inventory manager in a single unified app.
- 🔒 **Compulsory Customer Authentication:** Zero guest access. Customers register with Full Name, Email & Password, or 1-tap Continue with Google (powered by verified Google OAuth Client IDs).
- 📲 **In-App Direct OTA Updates (v3.2.4):** Pure dedicated `arm64-v8a` single-asset distribution preventing installation errors, first-time center changelog popup, and persistent top update banner.
- ☁️ **Google Firebase Cloud Sync:** Full integration with Google Firebase Firestore (`shalimar-store`) on the Free Spark Tier. Products, stock levels, and prices sync in real time across owner and customer phones.
- 🔔 **Dual-Tier Push & Local Notification Engine:**
  - *"🔔 Notify Me When in Stock":* Customers can subscribe to restock alerts (`restock_<productId>`) on out-of-stock items.
  - Automatic restock notification dispatch when the store owner toggles stock back in the Admin Panel.
  - Native local heads-up order dispatch alerts upon placing WhatsApp orders.
  - Store broadcast topic announcements (`store_broadcast`).
- 🌙 **Complete AMOLED Dark Theme Overhaul:** All screens (including Splash, Onboarding carousel, Login modal, and Customer decks) redesigned with adaptive semantic tokens for true `#121212` / `#1E1E1E` AMOLED aesthetics.
- 🧑‍💼 **Production Admin Hardening:** Pure live store inventory only — demo quick-loader stripped and hardcoded credentials purged for production security.
- 🔍 **Interactive Product Detail View:** Tap any card for full details, smooth hero animation, and multi-image swipeable carousel.
- 💡 **Smart Recommendations:** Dual horizontal carousels for *"More Like This"* (category alternatives) and *"More For You"* (staples).
- 🔤 **Plus Jakarta Sans Typography:** High-clarity geometric typography tailored for modern retail apps.
- ⚡ **Lightweight Performance:** Constrained image decoding caches (~85% RAM savings) and optimized shaders for smooth 60 FPS on budget phones.
- 🛒 **Persistent Shopping Cart:** Items stay safely saved in local storage until ordered on WhatsApp or cleared.
- 📦 **Split-per-ABI APKs:** Lightweight builds (~27 MB) signed with APK Signature Scheme v1–v4.
