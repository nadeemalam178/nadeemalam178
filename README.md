### 🏬 Flagship Project: [Shalimar Store (v4.2.0)](https://github.com/nadeemalam178/shalimar-store)

> **Since 1988 — Trust & Quality General Store Mobile App**  
> *Alamganj, Ashok Rajpath, Patna - 800007, Bihar*

**[Shalimar Store](https://github.com/nadeemalam178/shalimar-store)** is a production-grade, dual-mode general store & grocery mobile application built with Flutter & Dart. It brings traditional neighborhood shopping into the digital era with **zero gateway fees**, **compulsory customer registration & active Google sign-in**, **direct owner login (Shalimar@1988)**, **Google Gemini Pro AI Hub**, **direct WhatsApp ordering**, **zero-fee UPI payments**, **Google Firebase real-time cloud catalog sync**, **in-app direct OTA updates**, and an **ID & Password protected owner management panel**.

- ⚡ **Dual-Mode Architecture:** Customer catalog browsing + Owner/Admin inventory manager in a single unified app.
- ✨ **Google Gemini Pro AI Hub (v4.2.0):**
  - **📸 Vision "Photo-to-Product":** 1-tap snap of any grocery item automatically fills name, category, pack unit, MRP, and description.
  - **📢 Automated Customer Push Notifications:** Gemini crafts engaging Hinglish/English deals and festive announcements synced to Firestore and pushed to registered devices.
  - **🧾 Parchi & WhatsApp List Decoder:** Parses handwritten chits and unstructured WhatsApp grocery lists into itemized bills.
- 🚀 **Automatic Startup OTA Updates:** App instantly checks GitHub Releases on launch (on both Login and Main screens) and prompts one-tap download for device-matched rm64-v8a APK.
- 📲 **Instant App Sharing with QR Code:** High-contrast offline QR code in Admin Drawer & Customer Profile targeting direct APK downloads.
- 🛍️ **Customer Product Cards & Smart Kirana Descriptions:** 60% image flex ratio for high visual appeal + smart Hindi/English kirana descriptions.
- 🔐 **Compulsory Customer Authentication:** Zero guest access. Customers register with Full Name, Email & Password, or 1-tap Continue with Google.
- 🏢 **Store Owner Direct Access:** Dedicated Store Owner Login supporting Admin ID (Shalimar@1988) and password (Shal1mar@1988#).
- ☁️ **Google Firebase Cloud Sync:** Full integration with Google Firebase Firestore (shalimar-store) on the Free Spark Tier. Products, stock levels, and prices sync in real time across owner and customer phones.
- 🔔 **Dual-Tier Push & Local Notification Engine:** Customer restock subscriptions (estock_<productId>), store broadcast topic announcements (store_broadcast), and native heads-up alerts.
- 🌙 **Complete AMOLED Dark Theme Overhaul:** All screens redesigned with adaptive semantic tokens for true #121212 / #1E1E1E AMOLED aesthetics.
- 🔍 **Full-Screen Pinch-to-Zoom Image Viewer Everywhere:** Multi-image gallery in product details, cart, storefront photos, and admin dashboards with 1x-5x pinch zoom.
- 🛒 **Persistent Shopping Cart:** Items stay safely saved in local storage until ordered on WhatsApp or cleared.
- 📦 **Split-per-ABI APKs:** Lightweight builds (~27 MB) signed with APK Signature Scheme v1–v4.
